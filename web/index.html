    Make programmers out of ATtiny85 boards

    A Digispark or Franzininho has enough free pins for ISP only if its own RESET pin becomes an I/O pin (to drive the target's reset), and that takes a programmer. Two steps and two boards solve that: the first board gets DigiISP over USB and, while you hold the second board's RESET button, turns the second board into a complete programmer.

    1 Install DigiISP on the first board

    Over USB, through the Micronucleus bootloader the board comes with. No wiring.

    1. Unplug the board.
    2. Click Upload DigiISP; the device picker opens.
    3. Plug the board in. It appears in the picker as an unknown device (16d0:0753): select it and click Connect within about 5 seconds, before the bootloader gives up. Works with Micronucleus 1.x (new Digisparks) and 2.x.

      2 Turn the second board into a programmer

      The first board writes Micronucleus and DigiISP to the second board over ISP, verifies them, and only then sets the fuses that turn its RESET pin into I/O. You hold the second board's RESET meanwhile, about 15 seconds.

      Only the first board goes into USB.
